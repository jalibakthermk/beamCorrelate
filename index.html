Method notes

conventions and how each figure is computed

Conventions. H is positive to the right (R), V positive upward (U), seen from behind the lamp. ΔH/ΔV is the shift applied to the simulation so it lines up with the measurement. Type A/B IES files and CSV grids are read as H-V angles directly. Type C (IES/LDT) is converted assuming the optical axis at C0/γ90 with C90 to the right; tick "Mirror left/right" if a beam comes out flipped.

CSV. Matrix layout with H angles across the first row and V angles down the first column (tick "CSV rows are H" if transposed), or three columns H, V, cd. Comma, semicolon or tab separated.

Ratio. Measured / simulated, on a log₂ colour scale so ×2 and ×0.5 look equally far from ×1. RMS deviation and the tolerance band are computed on unmasked cells only.

Cut-off. Vertical scans in 0.05° steps on log-interpolated data. Sharpness G = log₁₀ I(β) − log₁₀ I(β+0.1°), taken at its maximum. The cut-off position β is the centre of the gradient peak (inflection of log I). Linearity is the spread of β across the scan positions. The elbow is where a straight-line fit of the rising shoulder meets the horizontal part, and the shoulder angle comes from that fit. G is only as good as the data resolution near the cut-off.

Gamma. For each measured cell above the threshold, γ = min √(|Δr|²/DTA² + ΔI²/ΔItol²) over nearby simulated positions, searched to 2·DTA (so γ is capped at 2). γ ≤ 1 passes: the simulation matches either in value or within the distance-to-agreement. ΔItol is a share of the local measured value (local) or of measured Imax (global). The plain-ratio pass rate is shown beside it; the gap between them is error that is only a small position shift.

SSIM. Structural similarity on log₁₀(I / mask threshold), so it weighs a 2:1 error the same at 500 cd and at 20 000 cd. Local means, variances and covariance use a Gaussian window of σw degrees. SSIM = l·c·s: luminance l compares local mean level, contrast c compares local variation, structure s is the local correlation of the two patterns. C₁ = (0.01·D)², C₂ = (0.03·D)², C₃ = C₂/2, with D the dynamic range above the mask in decades. Cells where both datasets are below the mask are not evaluated. A low s with l and c near 1 points to a shape difference: a lost striation, a moved kink or a smeared cut-off.

Contours. For each iso level, distances in degrees from every point of one contour to the nearest point of the other, both ways: mean, 95th percentile and maximum (Hausdorff). The signed shift is positive where the simulated line lies outside the measured one. Extents are the furthest cells at or above the level; * marks a window edge.

Calibration. Fits Ical = a·(GσH,σV ∗ Isim)(H−δH, V−δV) + halo + floor, where G is a Gaussian blur, the halo is the simulation blurred by 5° and the floor is uniform. Aim (δ), blur and the linear terms are fitted together because blur moves the apparent centre of an asymmetric beam. Each σ is labelled by how much the fit worsens when it is halved or raised by half: a beam without sharp features in one direction cannot determine σ in that direction.
